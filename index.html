If you use Milkman Ledger with Google Sign-In (Online Server / Gmail Cloud Sync) and wish to permanently delete your cloud account and all associated online data, you can submit a deletion request using the steps below.

1. How to Request Account & Cloud Data Deletion

  1. Click the button below or send an email from the Google (Gmail) address linked to your Milkman Ledger account.
  2. Send your request to ravvvinder@gmail.com with the subject line: Account Deletion Request — Milkman Ledger.
  3. Include your registered Gmail address and role (Milkman, Customer Online, or Supplier Online) so we can verify ownership of the account.
  4. You will receive a confirmation reply once your account and all cloud records have been permanently deleted (completed within 30 days of verification).

Warning: Deleting your cloud account is permanent and cannot be undone. Synced online records and Gmail cloud backups will no longer be recoverable.

Request Account Deletion via Email

2. Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

Upon completing your deletion request, Cyberfare Tech permanently deletes the following data from our Google Firebase servers:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account profile (Google email address and Firebase User ID).
  • Your cloud-synced dairy configuration (business/display name and optional UPI ID).
  • All synced customer/supplier ledger entries (milk sales, purchases, amendments, and payment records).
  • Masked contact lookup identifiers (******XXXX) and linked online account records.
  • Push notification history and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens.

3. Data Retention

No personal cloud records or ledger entries are retained after your deletion request is completed. Temporary automated server logs maintained by Google Firebase infrastructure expire automatically according to standard Google Cloud security retention schedules.

4. Deleting Local Data Stored on Your Device

Note on Offline/Local Data: Milkman Ledger stores full customer/supplier WhatsApp phone numbers, offline Room database entries, local PIN/security hashes, and internal backup snapshots strictly on your Android device. To remove all local data immediately:
  • Open Milkman Ledger → Settings to sign out of Google, unlink online accounts, or delete individual saved backups.
  • Uninstall Milkman Ledger (or go to Android Settings → Apps → Milkman Ledger → Storage → Clear Storage) to permanently erase all local on-device records.

5. Contact & Privacy Policy

For questions about account deletion or your privacy rights, email ravvvinder@gmail.com or review our Privacy Policy.
